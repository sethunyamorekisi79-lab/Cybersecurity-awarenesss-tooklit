# Phishing Awareness Guide
## What is Phishing?
Social Engineering attack where an attacker pretends to be a trusted entity in order to steal your credentials.
## 5 Signs of Phishing
1. Pressure - "Your account will be closed!"
2. Unusual sender - e.g. micros0ft-support@gmail.com
3. Hover over the link - Doesn’t match what is shown
4. Spelling mistakes - Bad grammar/spelling.
5. Requesting sensitive information - Ask for your password/OTP.
## What to do if you receive one?
1. Don’t click any links or download anything.
2. Report to IT/report@phishing.gov
3. Delete the email.
4. If you did click then change your password immediately and turn on MFA.
